I like Tom Cruise; he is brilliant. I had lost the desire to be a fighter pilot; watching Cruise in Top Gun (1986), I want to be a fighter pilot again. I have spectacles now - around negative 0.5 to 0.75, don't know the exact number. So being a fighter pilot in an institution is impossible unless I get a surgery, which I don't plan on getting. Maybe I will. But my plan is to skip all the institutional bloat and buy my own jet. Jared Isaacman - I think that's his name - has a private MiG-29; both Indian Air Force and the Indian Navy have MiG-29s but different variants. Jared Isaacman, read about him on the internet (i don't know how his name is spelt), he is actually a person I look up to. So I will buy one like he did. All this because of Tom Cruise. Love u Mr. Cruise. The 24 year old Cruise in Top Gun (1986) is the perfect archetype I want to be. He is fantastic, confident, beautiful, smart, intelligent and admirable and much more...

So yes, it is decided. Although I am now also, besides jets, interested in Bush Flying. Read about it on the internet "Bush Flying". It is fun. Very Fun.
