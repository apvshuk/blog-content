Her husband was guillotined 9 months ago. The court officials were responsible for the socio-economic troubles the state endured and which led to the French Rev. of 1789. The French, at that time had emptied their treasures in helping 13 American colonies to gain independence from the Brits. A bad yield followed a few years later and the revolt happened.
The revolt had both the king and the Queen captured after the king tried to shake hands with Austria to wage war on France so that he could get kingship again. This failed, Austria itself waged a war (there was another country I forget the name), and the then French govt. The Jocobin govt. actually used it to show its people that they are relevant. Because of this reason, they wanted to prolong the war. The Austrian side wanted the Queen back, because she was originally from Austria. But the Jacobin govt. didn't want to give her back, for this would cause peace and Jacobin govt. wouldn't be able to show itself relevant. The Queen was thus guillotined, and unnecessarily so. She could have been easily returned, but she was used as a political asset. Guillotining her made the war more aggressive. That's what they wanted, the Jacobins.
The leader of the Jacobin govt. got guillotined too later, i think in 1794 (not sure); and very deservedly so. Read about 'The reign of Terror' and you'll know why he deserved it.
